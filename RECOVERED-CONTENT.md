## Mission (homepage version)
> The Nepali Women's Global Network promotes networking, support and self-reliance among Nepali women through education, advocacy, service and collaboration with groups with similar missions. Our dreams and goals are viable and we need your support to meet our dreams and goals.

## Mission (About page version)
> The NWGN California Chapter works to connect and support Nepali women, helping them to become more self-reliant through education, advocacy, and service. The organization also collaborates with other groups that share similar goals and missions. The NWGN California Chapter believes that the dreams and goals of Nepali women are important and worth pursuing, and they welcome support from others in helping to make these dreams a reality. Through networking, education, and advocacy, the NWGN California Chapter aims to empower Nepali women and help them achieve success.

## About NWGN CA Chapter (About page)
> The NWGN California Chapter is a non-profit organization that is led by women and dedicated to supporting the development of Nepalese women. The organization focuses on providing a range of support, including financial assistance, emotional support, and social support, to help Nepalese women grow and achieve success. This support is provided through a variety of programs and activities that are designed to meet the unique needs of Nepalese women and help them achieve their goals. The NWGN California Chapter is committed to making a positive difference in the lives of Nepalese women and is an important resource for those looking to make a positive impact on the lives of women in Nepal.

## Goals (6)
1. To foster a supportive global network of friendship and understanding between Nepali women and other women.
2. To collaborate with national and international organizations with similar missions on events, activities and projects.
3. To represent and advocate for the interests of Nepali women and women in general.
4. To provide leadership training and opportunities to participate in the affairs of the organization.
5. To promote citizenship and civic involvement.
6. To support women in crisis through consultation networks, peer support, confidential social service referrals and resource pooling.

## Events & News archive

### International Women's Day 2024 Celebration (March 09, 2024)
> FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE: San Francisco, CA – NWGN-CA Chapter heartily invites you to celebrate International Women's Day 2024 on March 09th. IWD is celebrated worldwide on March 8th and has been one of our annual traditions since the organization's inception. Established in 2012, the NWGN California Chapter—a non-profit women-led organization in the San Francisco Bay Area—has been dedicated to supporting the growth of Nepalese women's development by providing financial, emotional, and social support. This year we adopted the UN theme: "Inspire Inclusion." The highlight of the event will be presentations by influential speakers who have made a remarkable contribution to the Nepali community. During our cultural event, talented and energetic artists will showcase their creativity.
> Sponsorship tiers: Silver $200+, Gold $300+, Platinum $500, Diamond $1,000. Funds raised contribute to women's empowerment activities.
> Contact: Laxmi Kharel, President; Pragya Wagle, General Secretary.

### Invitation to Women's Day 2023 Celebration (March 12, 2023)
> FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE: San Francisco, CA – The NWGN CA Chapter is proud to announce its celebration of International Women's Day 2023 on March 12th, held at the **Berkeley Adult School, beginning at 4:00 pm**. Theme: "DigitALL: Innovation and Technology for Gender Equality" #EmbraceEquity. Features presentations by influential speakers and a cultural event by Bay Area artists. **Ticket price $20.00; children under 11 free.** Sponsorship tiers: Silver $200+, Gold $300+, Platinum $500, Diamond $1,000.

### Walk-a-Thon 2022 (September 10, 2022)
> NWGN CA Chapter organized its first-ever Walk-A-Thon on September 10, 2022, Theme: "To Empower Women's Health and Well-Being." NWGN-CA team members, Bay Area community leaders and many individuals participated. Special guest: **Ms. Shailee Basnet**, coordinator of 'The Seven Summits Women Team' (the world's first female team to climb the highest mountain on each continent). Thanks to all sponsors and volunteers.

### International Women's Day 2022 (March 5, 2022 — 112th IWD)
> On March 5th, 2022, the NWGN CA Chapter celebrated its 112th International Women's Day via Zoom. Theme: "Gender equality today for a sustainable tomorrow." 2nd year celebrated via Zoom. Guest speakers: Gender & Media expert/journalism educator Dr. Samikshya Koirala; Psychologist Dr. Chori Laxmi Maharjan; Keynote — TV journalist and Gender Analyst Aarti Chataut. Joined by Bay Area community leaders via Zoom and Facebook Live.

### Virtual Leadership Training 2021 (December 11, 2021)
> On December 11, 2021 (PST), virtual training sessions were provided by the NWGN Global Team on Women in Leadership, Role and Responsibility, and effective communication. Trainers via Zoom: Sr. Advisor Dr. Bidhya Ranjit Manandhar, Immediate Past President Rukmini Karki, and Global President Attorney Avima Upreti.

### International Women's Day 2021 (March 6, 2021 — 111th IWD)
> On March 6th, 2021, the NWGN CA Chapter celebrated its 111th International Women's Day via Zoom. Theme: "Women in leadership: Achieving an equal future in a COVID-19 world." A historic 100% virtual event. Guest speakers: Dr. Sanju Adhikari, Attorney Sadhana Ghimire Bhetuwal, Attorney Rabina Shrestha. Joined via Zoom and Facebook Live.
