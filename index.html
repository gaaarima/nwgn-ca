A Community Built on
Sisterhood & Strength

The NWGN California Chapter is a women-led non-profit established in 2012, dedicated to supporting the growth and development of Nepalese women across the San Francisco Bay Area and beyond.

We provide financial assistance, emotional support, and social resources — helping Nepalese women achieve self-reliance, build lasting connections, and make their mark in society.

We collaborate with national and international organizations that share our vision, and actively advocate for the rights and interests of Nepali women everywhere.

We believe the dreams and goals of Nepali women are worth pursuing — and we exist to make sure nothing stands in the way. — NWGN California Chapter

Our Mission Pillars

Global Sisterhood

Foster a supportive global network of friendship and understanding between Nepali women near and far.

Collaboration

Partner with national and international organizations that share our vision for women's empowerment.

Advocacy

Represent and advocate for the rights and interests of Nepali women and women in general.

Leadership

Provide leadership training and opportunities, growing the next generation of Nepali women leaders.

Civic Engagement

Promote citizenship and active civic involvement within the Nepalese-American community.

Crisis Support

Support women in crisis through peer support, counseling referrals, and community resource pooling.

Privacy Policy

Effective September 14, 2026

The Nepalese Women's Global Network – California Chapter ("NWGN-CA," "we," "us," or "our") is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization based in the San Francisco Bay Area. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect the information you provide through our website.

Information We Collect

  • Membership registration: your full name, email address, phone number, and mailing address.
  • Donations: your name and email address, along with the donation amount.
  • Event sign-ups & ticket requests: your name and email address, and the event you're interested in.
  • Contact form: any information you choose to include in your message.
  • Photos: images submitted for our event gallery.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and manage memberships and donations.
  • To send you event announcements, updates, and confirmations you have requested.
  • To respond to your inquiries and provide member benefits (such as Lifetime member event tickets).
  • To operate, maintain, and improve our programs and website.

Payment Processing

Payments for memberships and donations are processed securely by Stripe. We do not collect or store your full card number, CVC, or banking details on our servers — that information is handled directly by Stripe under its own privacy policy.

Service Providers

We use trusted third-party services to run the site, and share only the information needed for each:

  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Formspree — secure handling of form submissions (membership, donation, contact, and event forms).
  • Cloudinary — hosting of event gallery photos.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone.

Cookies

Our website uses minimal cookies necessary for basic functionality. Third-party providers (such as Stripe) may set their own cookies during checkout. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Data Retention

We keep member and donor records for as long as needed to support our operations and meet legal or tax obligations as a non-profit, after which the information is securely deleted.

Your Choices & Rights

  • You may unsubscribe from our emails at any time using the link in any message or by contacting us.
  • You may request that we access, correct, or delete your personal information.

Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Effective" date above.
