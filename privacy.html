The Nepalese Women Global Network – California Chapter (“NWGN-CA,” “we,” “us”) is a non-profit organization based in the San Francisco Bay Area. This policy explains what information we collect through nwgnca.org, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us:

  • Membership sign-up: full name, email address, phone number, mailing address and the membership plan you choose.
  • Donations: your name, email address and donation amount.
  • Event updates & ticket requests: your email address, and for ticket requests your name, the event and number of tickets.
  • Contact form: your name, email address and anything you write in your message.
  • Event photos: photos taken at our events and shared in our gallery.

We do not collect your card or bank details. Payments are handled entirely by Stripe (see Donations & payments).

How we use your information

  • To process memberships and donations and send receipts.
  • To send event announcements and updates you asked for.
  • To reply to your questions and provide member benefits, such as Lifetime member tickets.
  • To keep records we are required to keep as a non-profit.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

Donations & payments

Donations and membership payments are processed by Stripe. When you pay, you enter your card details on Stripe’s secure checkout page, not on our website, and Stripe handles them under its own privacy policy. We receive only your name, email, the amount and a confirmation of payment.

We keep donation records so we can send receipts and meet our tax and reporting obligations. We do not publish donor names or amounts without your permission.

Membership fees include benefits (such as event tickets), so they may not be fully tax-deductible. Please check with your tax advisor.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of trusted services to run the website, and share only what each one needs:

  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Formspree — delivers form submissions (membership, donation, contact and event forms) to our email.
  • Cloudinary — hosts event gallery photos.
  • Google Fonts — serves the fonts used on this site.

We may also share information if required by law.

Cookies & tracking

Our website does not use advertising or analytics cookies, and we do not track you across other websites. Stripe may set its own cookies during checkout to keep payments secure. Because we do not track visitors, our site does not respond differently to browser “Do Not Track” signals.

How long we keep it

We keep member and donor records for as long as we need them to run our programs and meet legal and tax requirements, then delete them securely. You can ask us to delete your information sooner (see below).

Security

Our website uses an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and payment details are handled only by Stripe. Access to member and donor information is limited to board members who need it. No method of sending or storing data is 100% secure, but we take reasonable care to protect your information.

Your choices & rights

  • Unsubscribe from our emails at any time by replying to any message or contacting us.
  • See, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing us.
  • Photos: if you appear in a gallery photo and would like it removed, let us know and we will take it down.

Children

Our website is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and change the “Effective” date at the top.
